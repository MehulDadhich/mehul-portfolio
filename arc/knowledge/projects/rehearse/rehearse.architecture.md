# Rehearse: architecture

**Rehearse** works as a pipeline: **Setup** → **Voice** → **Interviewer** → **State machine** → **Proctoring** → **Debrief**. Setup: The candidate picks a role, seniority, style (behavioral, technical or mixed) and length, can paste a job description to tailor the questions, and tests the mic. Voice: The browser's Web Speech API speaks each question and transcribes the answer, sent after a short pause. If the browser's speech service is unreachable, the answer is recorded and transcribed on the server with Whisper large-v3-turbo. Interviewer: gpt-oss-120b on Groq replies with JSON: what to say, and whether it is a new question, a follow-up or the close. Every reply is checked against a JSON Schema and requested again if it is invalid or cut off. State machine: The FastAPI server, not the model, keeps the interview on track: it counts main questions, caps follow-ups at two per question, and tells the model when to move on or wrap up, so every interview ends on time. Proctoring: Leaving the tab gives a warning and the third ends the interview. Browser extensions that change the page, such as AI sidebars and writing assistants, are detected and block the start. Debrief: The full transcript is scored by the model as a judge, against a rubric: an overall score, five dimensions (clarity, depth, structure, relevance, evidence), notes per question, a stronger answer in the candidate's own words and what to practise next. Exportable as PDF.
