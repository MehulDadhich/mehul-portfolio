# RoadGuard: architecture

**RoadGuard** works as a pipeline: **Video in** → **Detect** → **Track** → **Rules** → **VLM** → **Incident**. Video in: RTSP cameras, webcams and files. Live sources reconnect with backoff when a camera drops, and a 5-second frame buffer in memory keeps recent frames for the VLM. Detect: Two YOLOv8s models merged into one class space: one fine-tuned on Indian highway cameras for cars, motorcycles, buses and trucks, and stock COCO weights for pedestrians and cyclists. A TensorRT INT8 engine runs when available, PyTorch FP16 otherwise. Track: ByteTrack keeps a persistent ID on every road user, and a track counts only after three confirming detections. Rules: Tier 1. A kinematic rules engine reads new box overlaps, sudden speed drops in km/h, vanishing vehicles and pile-ups, and fuses two different weak signals into one event. Events at confidence 0.6 or above alert immediately. VLM: Tier 2. Uncertain events, events involving pedestrians or two-wheelers, and a scan every 30 seconds go to a local Qwen3-VL-2B on its own thread. It answers only yes or no, so Tier 1 never waits for it. Incident: Each accident becomes one incident with at most one immediate alert and one VLM confirmation, logged with snapshots and sent to webhook or SMS endpoints on the local network. A health file reports the pipeline's status every 5 seconds.
