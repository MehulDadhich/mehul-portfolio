# RoadGuard lesson: Ask the VLM a smaller question

**Ask the VLM a smaller question.** Switching the verifier from a detailed JSON prompt to a yes/no question cut median VLM latency from 5.5 s to 0.47 s, and Tier 1 got its GPU time back.
