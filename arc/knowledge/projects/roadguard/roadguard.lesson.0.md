# RoadGuard lesson: One rule is never enough

**One rule is never enough.** In a real side impact the two boxes peaked at IoU 0.21, under the 0.3 contact threshold. A multi-signal rule now fuses two different weak cues, here a sudden stop and a vanishing vehicle 0.04 s apart, so impacts like that one are caught.
