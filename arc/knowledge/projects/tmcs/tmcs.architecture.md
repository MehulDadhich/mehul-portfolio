# Traffic Management & Control System: architecture

**Traffic Management & Control System** works as a pipeline: **CCTV / IP** → **Ingest** → **YOLO** → **Incidents** → **FastAPI** → **Operators**. CCTV / IP: Live RTSP streams from CCTV and IP cameras. Ingest: Continuous video ingestion with Python, OpenCV, MediaMTX and FFmpeg. YOLO: YOLOv8 and YOLO11 identify road users and flag stopped vehicles, accidents, fire, smoke and fallen objects. Incidents: Model detections are converted into incident events. FastAPI: Events flow through FastAPI REST endpoints into PostgreSQL. Operators: Monitoring dashboards and real-time alerts for downstream operators.
