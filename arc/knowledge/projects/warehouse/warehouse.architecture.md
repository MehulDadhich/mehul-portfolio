# Warehouse Monitoring System: architecture

**Warehouse Monitoring System** works as a pipeline: **RTSP cameras** → **YOLO11** → **ANPR** → **FastAPI** → **WebSocket** → **Dashboard**. RTSP cameras: Camera streams read with OpenCV, with health checks and downtime logs per camera. YOLO11: Detects and tracks vehicles and people in real time for counting and zone occupancy. ANPR: EasyOCR reads licence plates on vehicle entry and exit, with a confidence score on every read. FastAPI: REST APIs over PostgreSQL for vehicle and people records, camera status and alerts. WebSocket: Event streams push detections and alerts to the dashboard as they happen. Dashboard: React dashboards behind Nginx, with JWT/OAuth2 auth and roles for administrators, managers and operators.
